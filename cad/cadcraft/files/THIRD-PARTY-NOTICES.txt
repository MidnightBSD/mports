CADCraft bundled components

acadrust 0.6.5 is used unmodified for DWG import and export under its supplied
MPL 2.0 variant. Its Section 4 includes a named LEGAL file requirement that
is absent from the standard MPL 2.0 text.
Its corresponding source is available at:
https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/acadrust/0.6.5/download
https://github.com/hakanaktt/acadrust
LICENSE-acadrust.txt contains its full license, with escaped line endings
converted to ordinary line endings for readability.

Krilla 0.8.2 embeds ICC color profiles dedicated under CC0 1.0.
LICENSE-ICC-CC0.txt contains their full terms.

Egui's default fonts include Hack, Ubuntu Light, Noto Emoji, and emoji-icon-font.
Their full notices are installed alongside this file as Hack-Regular.txt,
UFL.txt, OFL.txt, and emoji-icon-font-mit-license.txt.
